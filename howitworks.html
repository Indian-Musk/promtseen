AI Image & Video Generation

promtseen includes built-in AI generators that let you create content without leaving the site. There are four modes — powered by cutting-edge, best-in-class AI engines.

Text → Image Most Used

Type a description and get a high-quality AI image in seconds. Perfect for concept art, social media visuals, thumbnails, and mockups.

  • Prompt input: Be descriptive — lighting, style, mood, subject.
  • Engine: Advanced photorealistic AI model.
  • Cost: 1 credit per generation.

Image → Image Transform

Upload a reference image, describe how you want it transformed, and the AI will re-imagine it. Great for style transfers, restyling photos, and creating variations.

  • Supports JPEG, PNG, WebP.
  • Upload directly from your device or paste an image URL.

Text → Video Popular

Describe a scene and generate a 3–40 second video. Choose your duration with the slider and let the AI produce motion.

  • Duration: 3s to 40s (1080p for ≤7s, 720p for ≤17s, 480p beyond).
  • Engine: Next-gen text-to-video AI with cinematic motion.
  • Cost: 1 credit per video task.

Image → Video & Keyframe Mode Advanced

Upload a start image (and optionally an end image) and generate a smooth video transition between them. Ideal for storytelling, product reveals, and before/after animations.

  • Single-image mode: animates one reference image.
  • Keyframe mode: uses two images to interpolate motion.
  • Reference URL mode: paste a YouTube / TikTok / Instagram link — we extract a thumbnail and animate it.

Why This Beats Everything Else

Free prompts become revenue machines. Even if a visitor never buys anything, they still click your affiliate card → you still get paid by the ad network. Combine paid prompts + ad affiliates + product affiliates for maximum income.

How the 2-Tab Click Works

Tab 1 — Primary URL

Your ad-network direct link (Monetag, Adsterra, etc.) opens here. This is where you earn.

Tab 2 — Secondary URL

Your prompt page or product page opens here. Visitor can buy, browse, or convert.

Both tabs open simultaneously the moment the visitor clicks your affiliate card. You earn from the ad network — promtseen never touches it.

Why 2 Tabs Instead of 1?

Ad networks often require their landing page to open in a new tab so it isn't blocked by browser pop-up rules. At the same time, we want your visitor to stay connected to your prompt — so they can still buy your prompt after the ad loads. That's why both open simultaneously:

  • Tab 1 (Ad Network): fires the ad click → you get paid.
  • Tab 2 (Prompt Page): keeps your content one click away → more chances to sell.

Both tabs open from a single user click, which is the most reliable way to avoid pop-up blockers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is promtseen free to use?

Yes! You get 5 free credits every day (when logged in) and 3 free guest generations per day without login. You can buy and sell prompts, create a channel, add affiliate links, and use the community features — all free. Paid features (like buying premium prompts or topping up credits) are optional.

How much can I earn as a seller?

Unlimited. You keep 80% of every prompt sale, 85% of every channel tip, and 100% of all ad-network affiliate earnings. Popular creators earn thousands of rupees per month. There's no cap on how much you can earn.

How do the ad-network affiliate links work?

Go to /affiliate.html. In the form, paste your Primary URL (your ad-network direct link — Monetag, Adsterra, PropellerAds, etc.) and your Secondary URL (your prompt page URL). When a visitor clicks the affiliate card on your prompt page, both URLs open in 2 new tabs — the ad network loads in Tab 1, your prompt page loads in Tab 2. You earn 100% of the ad-network revenue. promtseen takes 0% cut from ad-network earnings.

Which ad networks are supported?

Any ad network that provides a direct link works. Popular choices include Monetag, Adsterra, PropellerAds, PopAds, HilltopAds, AdMaven, and Clickadu — but you can use any network you prefer. Just paste the direct link in the Primary URL field.

When and how do I get paid?

For prompt sales and tips: once your available balance reaches ₹100, request a payout from your dashboard. Payments are processed via UPI or bank transfer.

For ad-network affiliates: your ad-network pays you directly on their own schedule — promtseen is not involved. Log in to your ad-network dashboard to check earnings and withdrawals.

Do I need a channel to sell prompts or add affiliates?

No — you can sell prompts and add affiliates without a channel. But having a channel gives you a public profile, subscribers, community posts, and the ability to receive tips. We strongly recommend creating one.

Can I upload videos, or only images?

Both! You can upload images (JPG, PNG, WebP — up to 5 MB) and video reels (MP4, WebM, QuickTime — up to 100 MB). Video reels play in our YouTube-Style Shorts player and support custom thumbnails.

What powers the AI generators?

Our image and video generators use advanced, industry-leading AI models that deliver photorealistic images and cinematic video output. Is my payment information secure?

Absolutely. All payments are processed by a leading PCI-DSS-compliant payment gateway. We never store your card details. UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and wallets are all supported.

Can I delete my prompts or account?

Yes. You can delete any prompt you've uploaded from your dashboard, and delete any affiliate link from /affiliate.html. For account deletion, contact our support team. Once deleted, prompts are removed from the marketplace, your channel, and all feeds.
